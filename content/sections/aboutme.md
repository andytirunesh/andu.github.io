I am a PhD candidate in **Agricultural & Resource Economics** at the University of Alberta, a **consultant with The World Bank**, and a **Doctoral Fellow at the Canadian Agri‑Food Policy Institute (CAPI)**.

My research sits at the intersection of **infrastructure**, **factor markets**, and **structural transformation** in low‑income countries. I use **causal inference**, **geospatial analysis**, and **administrative & survey data** to study how market access and public programs shape productivity and labor reallocation.

Prior to my PhD, I earned an MSc in Economics from the **Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences** and an MSc in **Agricultural & Food Economics** from the **University of Bonn** (Erasmus Mundus). I also hold an MSc and a BA in Economics from **Addis Ababa University**.
